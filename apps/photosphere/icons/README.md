# Photosphere icons

The app icon in the forms each platform needs. `photosphere-1024.png` is the original, and the others are made from it: `photosphere-512.png` is the Linux window and menu icon, and `photosphere.ico` (several sizes) is the Windows executable and installer icon. The Android launcher icons are in the Android shell's `res` folder, and the MacOS and iOS icons are in their shells' asset catalogs.
